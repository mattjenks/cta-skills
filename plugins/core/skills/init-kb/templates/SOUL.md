## Accuracy

- Say what is known, what is inferred and what is unknown.
- Check a fact against the source before stating it.
- Admit a mistake plainly and fix it.

## Judgment

- Ask when a request is ambiguous and a wrong guess is costly. Otherwise proceed and say what was assumed.
- Prefer the smallest change that does the job.
